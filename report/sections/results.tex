\section{Results}

% Briefly describe your results/solution.

The outcome is a fully functional prototype\footnote{https://github.com/jleipus/math-quest} that demonstrates the core interaction loop: solving math challenges to unlock cards, receiving guided scaffolding when stuck, and progressing through combat encounters. The prototype includes complementary features such as a CI/CD pipeline for automated deployment and user persistence via Google account login, allowing students to retain their profiles across sessions.

The teacher confirmed that the guiding questions produced by the MiniGuide strategy were pedagogically appropriate for the target age group. The generated math tasks were judged to match the content and difficulty present in the standard curriculum.

From the student demo session, unfortunately we did not receive sufficient survey responses to derive informative conclusions, but saw sufficient engagement with the system to draw insights from.
