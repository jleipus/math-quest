% Briefly describe your [expected] results/solution.

The expected outcome is a functional prototype that demonstrates the core interaction loop: solving math challenges to unlock cards, receiving guided scaffolding when stuck, and progressing through combat encounters. The prototype is not expected to be a production-ready tool, but rather a proof of concept that validates whether the combination of RAG-based scaffolding and game mechanics produces a coherent and engaging learning experience.

From the teacher evaluation session, we expect to learn whether the MiniGuide strategy produces pedagogically appropriate guidance — specifically, whether the guiding questions are relevant to the problem at hand and successfully avoid revealing direct answers. From the student demo session, we expect qualitative insights into whether the game loop feels engaging, whether the help agent is perceived as useful, and whether the damage mechanic strikes an appropriate balance between challenge and frustration.

These findings will inform whether the proposed architecture warrants further development, and identify which components — scaffolding logic, difficulty calibration, or game balance — require the most refinement.
