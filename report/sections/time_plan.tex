The project operates under a compressed five-week timeline, structured around one user testing session during the demo day with students and at least one separate session with a teacher.

During the first two weeks, the goal is to finalize the RAG pipeline integration with Matteboken.se content, implement core scaffolding logic and basic user model, and develop initial game mechanics. Progress, including preliminary results will be presented, addressing how theories and responsible design principles have been applied.

During the following 2--3 weeks, at least one testing session involving the stakeholder will take place, which will provide data through interaction logs and a short questionnaire. Additionally, pedagogical feedback on scaffolding quality and MiniGuide behavior will be collected. Collected data will be analyzed, and required improvements will subsequently be implemented.

A final testing session involving both the stakeholder and the students will take place upon the demo day, which will also provide valuable insights into necessary future improvements.
