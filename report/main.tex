    \begin{abstract}
        Mathematics classrooms often struggle to support students with widely different skill levels, leading to disengagement for some and anxiety for others. Standard AI chatbots can worsen this by providing direct answers, bypassing the reasoning process that learning depends on. This project presents the design of a gamified web-based tutoring system for primary school students aged 10--12, where mathematical challenges are embedded in a turn-based card combat game. The system uses a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture constrained by the \enquote{MiniGuide} pedagogical strategy, designed so that the AI provides guiding questions rather than direct answers, functioning as a supportive peer that aims to keep learners within their Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD). An exploratory evaluation with a teacher and students evaluates whether the interaction design produces appropriate pedagogical guidance and an engaging learning experience.
    \end{abstract}
